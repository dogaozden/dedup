The M5 browser suite: what it did before this branch repaired it, what it
does now, and what it does when the browser is made unreachable on purpose.

The middle one is the milestone's claim. The first and the last are what make
that claim worth anything: a suite that was reporting eighteen differing checks
while testing nothing, and a suite that now refuses to call a case green when
it never reached the screen the case is about.


=== 1. the script as of main@fbc196d, against this branch's binary ===
$ python3 <git show fbc196d:...m5_end_to_end.py> rust/target/release/dedup-rs <scratch>
18 check(s) differing
EXIT=1
(every pty case timed out and was killed; none of them was on the browser)

=== 2. the repaired script, both builds, unmodified ===
this branch    0 check(s) differing   EXIT=0   NOT REACHED lines: 0   'reached the browser' checks: 9
main@fbc196d   0 check(s) differing   EXIT=0   NOT REACHED lines: 0   'reached the browser' checks: 9

=== 3. the same script with only the menu Enter suppressed ===
$ sed 's/os.write(master, ENTER)/pass/' ... && python3 <that copy>
26 check(s) differing
EXIT=1   NOT REACHED lines: 9

per case, with the browser unreachable:
  confirmed deletion moves exactly the marked copies to the  reports a differing check
  cancelling the confirmation removes nothing                reports a differing check
  quitting with copies marked removes nothing                reports a differing check
  --hard deletes permanently and puts nothing in the Trash   reports a differing check
  space on every copy in a group cannot empty it             reports a differing check
  removing a hardlinked record removes every name it wears   reports a differing check
  bulk keep-shortest keeps the copy --auto shortest keeps    reports a differing check
  a terminal too small to lay out will not confirm a deletio reports a differing check
  a group the cache vouched for is re-read, disbelieved and  reports a differing check
  -i without a terminal refuses instead of guessing          all ok

The one case that still reports all ok is `-i without a terminal refuses
instead of guessing`, which is the only case in the file that never opens a
pty: it is a plain subprocess with no terminal at all, so suppressing the menu
Enter cannot reach it. Every case that drives a terminal now fails.
