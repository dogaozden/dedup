The bracket refuses 100ms, 500ms, 900ms and 60s. One second is refused
for a different reason: at ten readings wide, 'half a window into a
stall' falls between readings and the decay-shape assertion cannot
resolve it — so the tests also say the window must be several times the
reading spacing to mean anything. Two seconds passes, and should: the
spec allows it. The exact choice of three is a tuning judgement, and no
test here defends it.
